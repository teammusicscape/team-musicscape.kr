The project

A space where cultures resonate.

MUSICSCAPE — Resonant Landscape explores music as a meeting place for different ways of seeing and listening.

Audinos Guitar Duo brings together Korean guitarist-composer In Choi and Finnish guitarist-composer Paul-Erik Söderqvist. Their collaboration began in 2017 with concerts in Seoul and Jeju.

Rooted in nature, daily life and cultural memory, their works respond to the environments that shaped them. The project does not place two cultures side by side; it lets them overlap, question and transform one another through sound.
